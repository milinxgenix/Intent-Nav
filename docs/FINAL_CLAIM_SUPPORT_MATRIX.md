## 1. Technical Scope & Evidentiary Boundary Notice

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Engineering Scope & Evidentiary Boundary Notice:**
> - This matrix constitutes an **engineering evidence baseline** tracing technical patent claim limitations to concrete source code, automated test suites, and empirical browser artifacts.
> - It does **not** assert statutory patentability, novelty, non-obviousness, priority entitlement, or legal claim validity.
> - Where a claim is designated as **`EMPIRICALLY_DEMONSTRATED`** or **`BROWSER_E2E_TESTED`**, that empirical demonstration is strictly limited to the executed scenarios (Scenarios A–K, N1–N14) on the tested mock-site environments (Sites A–D in Google Chrome 153.0.8010.53 over CDP).
> - This empirical demonstration does **not** constitute a universal proof across arbitrary or uninspected third-party websites.
> - Zero synthetic shortcuts, zero hardcoded DOM element IDs, zero mock bypasses, and zero fabricated timestamps were utilized in the empirical evidence generation.
